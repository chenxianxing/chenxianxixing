Frequently Asked Questions

Why is WebP usually smaller than JPEG?

WebP uses a more modern encoding algorithm and is often smaller than JPEG at similar visual quality, making it ideal for web image optimization. However, some older browsers have limited WebP support, so confirm compatibility with your target environment before publishing.

What does the max width limit do?

Limiting the max width scales the image down proportionally, which is one of the most effective ways to reduce file size. For example, scaling a 4000px-wide original down to 1600px drastically cuts the pixel count and noticeably shrinks the file.

Does compression cost image sharpness?

JPEG and WebP are lossy: the lower the quality and the smaller the size, the more noticeable the loss. PNG is lossless and mainly shrinks the file by reducing dimensions, introducing no quality loss, but it offers relatively limited compression room.
