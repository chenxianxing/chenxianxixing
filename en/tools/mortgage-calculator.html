For a combined loan, the commercial and provident fund portions are calculated separately and then added together. If rates change, the latest figures published by the bank prevail; results from this tool are for budgeting reference only.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between equal installment and equal principal?

With equal installment, the monthly repayment is fixed and interest makes up a larger share early on while principal is smaller; with equal principal, a fixed amount of principal is repaid each month, the monthly payment decreases month by month, and total interest is lower, but early repayment pressure is greater. For example, for a 1 million loan over 30 years at 3.1% annual interest, total interest is about 537,000 with equal installment versus about 466,000 with equal principal.

How is a combined loan calculated?

A combined loan consists of a commercial loan and a provident fund loan. Each part is calculated with its own principal, rate and the same term, and the monthly payments are then added together. This tool lets you set the amount and rate for each part independently.

Are the calculation results accurate?

The calculations use standard financial formulas and run locally in your browser, matching the bank algorithms for equal installment/equal principal. Actual repayment follows the bank contract (rates, repayment dates and grace periods may differ slightly), so results are for budgeting reference only.
