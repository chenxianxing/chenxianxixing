Frequently Asked Questions

Does the order of lines change after deduplication?

Deduplication uses a "keep first occurrence" strategy: it scans top to bottom, keeps the first appearance of each line, and removes later duplicates. So lines keep their original order and are not re-sorted.

Does find/replace support regular expressions?

Yes. After enabling "Regular expression", the find box is treated as a JavaScript regex, and the replace box can use capture-group references like $1, $2. Enabling "Ignore case" matches case-insensitively. Each replacement shows the replacement count.

Can operations be undone, and where can results be saved?

All line operations are pushed onto a history stack. Click "Undo last step" to step back, or "Reset" to clear the input and the entire history. The finished result can be copied to the clipboard with one click, or downloaded as a .txt file.
