Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between HEX, RGB, HSL and HSV?

HEX and RGB are essentially the same: both represent colors with red, green and blue channels, and HEX is just a hexadecimal shorthand. HSL uses hue, saturation and lightness, while HSV uses hue, saturation and value. These models match human color perception more intuitively and make color adjustment easier.

How do I represent colors with transparency?

Add an alpha channel to RGB or HSL: rgba(r,g,b,a) or hsla(h,s,l,a), where a ranges from 0 to 1. This tool provides an opacity slider and outputs rgba/hsla in real time.

How is the complementary color calculated?

In the HSL/HSV color space, rotating the hue by 180 degrees gives the complementary color, which sits directly opposite on the color wheel and is often used for color contrast. This tool also provides light and dark variants.
