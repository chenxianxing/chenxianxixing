Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Pomodoro work rhythm?

By default, after 25 minutes of focus you take a 5-minute short break, and after every 4 focus cycles you take a 15-minute long break, then it restarts. You can adjust the focus, short-break and long-break durations, as well as the number of focus sessions that trigger a long break.

Does the alert sound require permissions or external files?

No. The alert uses the browser's built-in Web Audio API to synthesize a short "ding" in real time. It loads no external audio files and requests no notifications or microphone permissions. The AudioContext is created after your first click on "Start", complying with the browser's autoplay policy.

Is the timing accurate? Does it drift in a background tab?

Timing is based on the real time difference from Date.now(), not on accumulated timers, so the countdown stays accurate and never accumulates drift even if the tab is throttled or sent to the background.
