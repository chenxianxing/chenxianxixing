Frequently Asked Questions

What scenarios is image stitching good for?

It is often used to arrange chat logs, tutorial screenshots, product details or photo series into one long image for easy saving and sharing, and also works well for merging multiple portrait screenshots into a continuous vertical image.

What does "use largest side" mean?

For vertical stitching, all images are scaled to the same largest width before merging, avoiding misalignment from differing widths. For horizontal stitching, heights align to the largest height to keep seams even.

Will the stitched image become blurry?

Stitching only arranges the source pixels and does not reduce sharpness. However, when unifying size, upscaling small images to the target dimension may look less clear due to insufficient source resolution. Prefer "use largest side" instead.
