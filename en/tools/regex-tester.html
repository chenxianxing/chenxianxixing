Common Regex Cheat Sheet

Frequently Asked Questions

Are regex test results uploaded to a server?

No. All matching is performed locally in your browser with the JavaScript RegExp engine, and neither the regular expression nor the test text ever leaves your device.

What do the flags g, i, m and s mean?

g means global matching (find all matches instead of only the first); i means ignore case; m means multiline mode (^ and $ match the start and end of each line); s means the dot matches newline characters; u means Unicode mode; y means sticky matching (matching starts from lastIndex).

What do $1 and $& mean in replacements?

$1 to $9 refer to the contents of capture groups 1 through 9; $& means the entire matched text; $` means the content before the matched text; $' means the content after the matched text; $$ means a literal dollar sign.
