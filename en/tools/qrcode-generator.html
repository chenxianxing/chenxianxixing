Frequently Asked Questions

What does the QR code error correction level do?

The error correction level determines how likely a QR code remains scannable after being obscured or damaged. L tolerates about 7% damage, M about 15%, Q about 25% and H about 30%. Use a higher error correction level when there is a lot of content or when printing on uneven surfaces.

Can the generated QR codes be used commercially?

Yes. QR codes themselves carry no fee; this tool generates them entirely in your local browser and neither collects nor uploads any content. The results can be freely used for commercial or personal purposes.

Is a bigger QR code always clearer?

Clarity depends mainly on content density and print resolution rather than size alone. More content means denser modules, so it is recommended to increase the size appropriately and set a margin to ensure reliable scanning.
