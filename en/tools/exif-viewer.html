Frequently Asked Questions

Can EXIF information leak privacy?

Yes. A JPEG photo's EXIF may contain precise GPS location, capture time and device model. Sharing the original file directly can expose your whereabouts and device details. Consider clearing location, time and device information with this tool before sharing.

Why does it say no EXIF information was detected?

There are three possible reasons: the file is not in JPEG format (PNG and WebP contain no EXIF); the photo was screenshotted, exported from an editor or compressed by social media, which removes metadata; or the device had location recording turned off.

Can I still view a photo after clearing EXIF?

Yes. Clearing only removes the metadata segment and does not change the pixel content, so image quality, dimensions and format are all unaffected. The photo can be viewed, printed and shared normally.
