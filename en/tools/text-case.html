Case Converter

Online letter case and naming convention converter that supports UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, swap case, plus camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT_CASE. Multi-select and combine in order, with real-time preview.

Note: multiple modes can be checked at once and are applied in this order: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, swap case, camel, pascal, snake, kebab, CONSTANT_CASE. Naming convention modes first split the text into words (including camelCase boundaries and symbol separators) and then recombine them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can multiple conversions be combined?

Yes. All mode buttons support multi-select and conversions are applied in the order listed on the page. For example, choosing lowercase first and then snake_case will normalize any text into underscore naming.

What is the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?

In camelCase (lower camel case), the first word starts lowercase and each subsequent word starts uppercase, e.g. myVariableName. In PascalCase (upper camel case), every word starts uppercase, e.g. MyVariableName. Both are common naming conventions in different programming languages.

What is CONSTANT_CASE?

CONSTANT_CASE, also known as upper snake case, uses all uppercase letters with words separated by underscores, e.g. MAX_COUNT. It is commonly used for naming constants, enum values or configuration items.
