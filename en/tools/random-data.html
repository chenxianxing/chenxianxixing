Random Data Generator

Online bulk random test data generator supporting many fields such as Chinese names, phone numbers, emails and ID numbers, exportable as one item per line, CSV, JSON or with a custom delimiter, all generated locally in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the generated ID numbers real and valid?

No. ID numbers are randomly generated by an algorithm with a correct check digit, but the corresponding numbers do not necessarily exist. They must never be used for any real business or to impersonate others, and are for testing only.

Which export formats are supported?

Four formats are supported: one item per line (space-separated), CSV (with header), JSON (array of objects), and a custom delimiter (you specify the separator).

How many rows can be generated at most?

Up to 1000 rows can be generated at a time, which covers the vast majority of API testing, form load testing and sample data needs.
