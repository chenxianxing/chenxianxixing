Frequently Asked Questions

What do the five fields of a Cron expression mean?

A standard Cron expression has 5 fields: minute (0-59), hour (0-23), day of month (1-31), month (1-12) and day of week (0-7, where both 0 and 7 mean Sunday), separated by spaces. This tool also supports a 6-field form that adds a seconds field (0-59) at the front.

What special syntax is supported?

Supported: *, numbers, a-b ranges, */n and a-b/n steps, a,b,c lists, month names JAN-DEC and weekday names SUN-SAT; the day field supports ?, L (last day of month) and W (nearest weekday), and the weekday field supports 5#2 (the second Friday of the month); the special macros @yearly, @monthly, @weekly, @daily, @hourly and @reboot are also supported, where @reboot is informational only and is not parsed precisely.

How are the next run times calculated?

Starting from the current time, the tool advances day by day and matches the month, day and weekday conditions, then combines hours, minutes (and seconds) into candidate times, searching up to 4 years ahead to list the next 10 runs. All computation is done locally in your browser and the expression is never uploaded.
