      Frequently Asked Questions

      How accurate are MBTI test results?

      MBTI tests use self-report scales to measure tendencies across four dimensions. The results are for entertainment and self-exploration and can help you see yourself from a new angle, but they are not a basis for professional psychological assessment or hiring.

      Is my test data uploaded to a server?

      No. All answers and scoring are computed locally in your browser, nothing is uploaded to any server, the data disappears when you close the page, and no sign-up is required.

      What do the four letters mean?

      E/I stands for energy (Extraversion/Introversion), S/N for information (Sensing/Intuition), T/F for decision-making (Thinking/Feeling), and J/P for lifestyle (Judging/Perceiving). The four letters combine into 16 personality types, such as INTJ Architect.
