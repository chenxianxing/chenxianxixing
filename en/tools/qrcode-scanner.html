Frequently Asked Questions

Does QR code scanning require an internet connection?

No. This tool decodes 100% locally in your browser. Neither images nor camera footage are uploaded to any server, so it works even when you are offline.

What types of QR code content are supported?

Supported formats include URLs, WiFi, vCards (vCard/MECARD), phone numbers, emails, SMS, locations, and plain text. After scanning, content is automatically categorized and displayed in a structured way.

What should I do if the camera cannot be started?

The camera is only available in an HTTPS secure context or on localhost. If the page is opened via file:// or accessed over an insecure network, the browser will block camera access. In that case, use the upload image or paste screenshot mode instead.
