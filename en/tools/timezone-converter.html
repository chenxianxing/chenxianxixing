Frequently Asked Questions

Which time zones does the world clock support, and where does the data come from?

About 50 popular cities and the full IANA time zone dropdown are built in, covering UTC-12 to UTC+14. All times are computed locally with the browser's Intl.DateTimeFormat and the IANA time zone database, with no external requests. Clocks refresh every second, and the time slider lets you view any moment of the day.

How does the time converter translate a source-zone time into target zones?

The source wall time is first combined with that zone's UTC offset to reconstruct an exact UTC instant, then formatted for each target zone. Daylight saving time is handled automatically, so for example the New York to Beijing difference changes from 13 to 12 hours during DST, with no manual adjustment.

How does the tool tell whether a zone observes daylight saving time?

It compares the zone's UTC offset in January and July: if the two differ, the zone observes DST and the clock card shows a DST badge; when the current offset matches the summer offset, the zone is currently in daylight saving time.
