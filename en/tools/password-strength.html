    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does this tool save or upload the password I enter?

    No. All checks are performed locally in your browser via JavaScript; the password is neither uploaded to any server nor written to local storage or cookies. You can even use it with the network disconnected. Clearing the input box after checking is recommended.

    What does entropy (bits) mean?

    Entropy measures the randomness of a password, in bits. Roughly speaking, entropy = length × log2(character pool size). The higher the entropy, the more attempts a brute-force attack needs. Above 60 bits is generally considered strong and above 128 bits extremely strong. Note that this tool also deducts effective entropy for patterns such as repeats and sequential runs.

    Is the crack time estimate accurate?

    The estimate assumes attacker guess rates: offline fast hashing around 10 billion per second, offline slow hashing (e.g. bcrypt) around 10 thousand per second, and online brute force around 100 per hour. The results are for security reference only; the reality depends on the attacker's computing power and the target's protection strategy.
