Frequently Asked Questions

What does locking the ratio do when cropping?

After locking the ratio, dragging the crop handles keeps the aspect ratio fixed, which suits fixed-size needs like avatars (1:1), covers (16:9), or portrait wallpapers (9:16), avoiding distorted crops.

Does rotating or flipping reduce image quality?

Rotation and flipping are purely geometric pixel transforms and do not lose sharpness by themselves. Blur only appears if you export JPEG at very low quality or upscale a low-resolution source.

Why does the exported JPEG background turn white?

JPEG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. To keep a transparent background, export as PNG or WebP.
