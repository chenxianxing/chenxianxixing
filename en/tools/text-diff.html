Text Compare

Online text difference tool that compares two texts line by line using the LCS algorithm. Deleted lines are highlighted in red, added lines in green, with line numbers shown. Supports ignoring whitespace and ignoring case, counts added, deleted and unchanged lines, and lets you copy a difference report.

Frequently Asked Questions

What algorithm does the text comparison use?

It uses the LCS (longest common subsequence) dynamic programming algorithm for line-by-line comparison, finding the minimum edit path between the two texts and presenting differences in three states: deleted, added and unchanged. Calculation runs in real time when there are fewer than 2000 lines.

What do Ignore whitespace differences and Ignore case do?

With Ignore whitespace differences enabled, lines with identical content but different numbers of spaces, tabs or line breaks are treated as the same. With Ignore case enabled, lines that differ only in letter case are also treated as the same, letting you focus on real content changes.

Can I export or copy the difference result?

Yes. Clicking Copy difference report copies a plain-text report with + and - markers and statistics to the clipboard, ready to paste into chats, documents or commit records.
