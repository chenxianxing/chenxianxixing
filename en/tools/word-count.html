Frequently Asked Questions

How is the word count calculated?

Chinese characters are counted one by one (each Han character counts as 1), and English words are counted as consecutive strings of letters or digits (each word counts as 1). The total is the sum of both. The character count includes all visible characters, with or without spaces and line breaks.

Are the statistics uploaded to a server?

No. All statistics are computed locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to any server, so you can use it with confidence.

Does it support counting .txt files?

Yes. You can drag a .txt or .md file directly into the input box; the tool reads the file and counts it automatically. Files must be smaller than 5MB.

How is reading time estimated?

It is estimated at roughly 300 characters per minute, a typical adult Chinese reading speed; mixed Chinese and English content is converted by total word count. The result is for reference only.
