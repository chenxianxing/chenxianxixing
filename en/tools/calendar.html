Frequently Asked Questions

Which years of holiday data does the calendar tool support?

Public holiday arrangements for 2024 to 2027 are built in, covering New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Qingming Festival, Labour Day, Dragon Boat Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day, including holiday dates and makeup workdays. Dates from 2028 onward display normally but without holiday labels, until data can be added once official notices are published.

How are workdays and holidays counted in the monthly statistics?

Workdays are Monday to Friday dates outside holiday ranges, plus weekend dates marked as makeup workdays. Holidays are dates within official holiday ranges and may overlap with weekends. Weekends are Saturdays and Sundays. The three counts can add up to more than the days in the month because holidays and weekends overlap.

What keyboard shortcuts are available?

When the page body has focus (shortcuts are inactive while an input is focused), press the left arrow ◀ for the previous month, the right arrow ▶ for the next month, and the letter T to jump back to the current month with today selected.
