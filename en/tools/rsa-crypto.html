All operations run locally, and the private key is never uploaded. Ideal for learning, API integration testing, and protecting short text.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long a text can RSA encrypt?

RSA-OAEP can only encrypt short data in a single operation. With a 2048-bit key, the maximum plaintext is about 190 bytes (roughly 190 English characters or about 60 Chinese characters). For long text, switch to AES symmetric encryption and use RSA to encrypt the AES key.

What are the public and private keys used for?

The public key is used to encrypt or verify signatures and can be published openly. The private key decrypts or generates signatures and must be kept secure. Encryption uses the recipient's public key, and only the holder of the matching private key can decrypt and recover the content.

Are the generated keys uploaded to a server?

No. The key pair is generated locally in your browser via WebCrypto, with no network requests made during the process. The private key never leaves your device. Please keep the downloaded PEM files safe on your machine.
