Frequently Asked Questions

What is nine-grid cropping used for?

Slice one image into nine tiles (3x3) and post them in order to social platforms so they reassemble into the full picture in the grid, a very common trick. This tool also breaks long images or posters into multiple grid assets.

What is the difference between "center square crop" and "equal slicing by original ratio"?

"Center square crop" first trims the longer side of the source, takes the largest centered square, then divides it evenly, ideal for non-square sources. "Equal slicing by original ratio" does not crop; it slices directly by the source's width and height ratio, keeping the full picture but tiles may not be square.

Why download tile by tile instead of a ZIP?

To keep everything running entirely in the browser with no extra libraries or external requests, this tool triggers downloads one by one. Your browser may ask whether to allow multiple downloads; just choose allow. File names increment like original-1, original-2.
