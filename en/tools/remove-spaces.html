Frequently Asked Questions

Can multiple modes be checked at the same time?

Yes. All modes can be checked and are applied in a fixed order: first trim leading and trailing whitespace, then remove tabs, collapse consecutive spaces and remove all spaces, and finally handle line breaks and empty lines, then remove all whitespace. Combine them as needed.

What is the difference between Remove all whitespace and Remove all spaces?

Remove all spaces only deletes half-width space characters, while Remove all whitespace deletes every whitespace character, including spaces, tabs (Tab) and line breaks. It is often used to compress multi-line text into a single line with no gaps.

Where can I see the character and line count changes after processing?

Above the result you will see the character and line counts before and after processing, for example 1200 characters to 1100 characters, so you can see the cleaning effect at a glance.
