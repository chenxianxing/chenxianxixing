Frequently Asked Questions

      

        
Why does the generated favicon.ico embed PNG data?

        
The ICO container allows embedded PNG images, which all modern browsers can parse. Compared with traditional BMP bitmaps, PNG offers better compression, an alpha channel and anti-aliasing, so this tool packs 16x16, 32x32 and 48x48 PNGs into a single favicon.ico for both quality and compatibility.

      

      

        
Will my uploaded image be sent to a server?

        
No. Image reading, scaling, icon rendering and ZIP packaging all happen locally in your browser with no network requests, so it works offline too.

      

      

        
How do I use the generated icons on my website?

        
Put favicon.ico, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png and manifest.json in your website root directory, then copy the generated HTML code into the <head> section of your pages. The name, short_name, theme_color and background_color in manifest.json are filled in from the values you enter.
